Privacy Policy

Daily Challenge Tracker · Last updated 17 August 2026

The short version

Daily Challenge Tracker does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What the app stores, and where

Your challenges, your daily task lists, the days you tick off, your notes and your reminder settings are saved in the app's own private storage on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. We have no server that receives them and no way to see them.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently. There is no cloud backup, so if you remove the app or reset your phone the data is gone.

Data we collect

None. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any other identifier
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files or any other content on your device
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers — the app contains no ads

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications — to show your daily reminder at the time you choose. The reminders are scheduled entirely on your device; no server is involved and nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Since it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Deleting your data

Delete a challenge inside the app to remove it and its history, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Because nothing is stored outside your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request you need to make.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds optional accounts or syncing, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will make clear what would be sent and when.
